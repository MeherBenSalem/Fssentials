## Posting checklist

- Upload the MP4 as a Reel and set `cover.jpg` as the cover. Keep the original audio; a trending track on top would
  bury the voiceover.
- Put the Modrinth link in your bio before posting, since the caption and the video's last frame point there.
- Paste `caption.txt` (the Instagram caption above).
- Add the alt text under Advanced settings.
- Post the pinned comment right after publishing and pin it.
- Reply to the first comments quickly; questions about features are a good reason to post a follow-up video.
- Cross-post the same file to TikTok and YouTube Shorts with their captions above.
